FUTURE_DS_01 — Business Sales Performance Analysis

📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing a Superstore sales dataset to understand business performance through sales, profit, orders, product performance, category performance, and regional trends.

The analysis was completed as part of Future Interns — Data Science & Analytics Task 1.

The project includes data analysis, business insights, visualizations, and a dashboard created using Google colab.

---

🎯 Objective

The main objectives of this analysis are to:

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Identify sales trends across different years.
- Identify top-selling products.
- Analyze sales and profit by product category.
- Analyze sales and profit across different regions.
- Identify important business patterns and performance areas.
- Present the findings through a professional dashboard.

---

📁 Dataset

The project uses a Superstore sales dataset containing information related to:

- Sales
- Profit
- Orders
- Quantity
- Products
- Categories
- Regions
- Sales years

The dataset was organized and analyzed to generate business performance insights.

🔍 Analysis Performed

1. Sales Trend by Year

Sales were analyzed across the available years to identify changes in business performance.

- 2014: 484,247.50
- 2015: 470,532.51
- 2016: 609,205.60
- 2017: 733,215.26

The analysis shows an overall increase in sales toward 2017.

2. Top-Selling Products

The top-selling products were analyzed based on total sales.

The Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier generated the highest sales among the products analyzed.

3. Sales by Category

Sales were compared across the major product categories:

- Technology
- Furniture
- Office Supplies

Technology generated the highest sales among the categories analyzed.

4. Sales by Region

Regional sales performance was analyzed to identify high-performing and lower-performing regions.

The West region recorded the highest sales among the regions analyzed.

5. Profit by Category

Profit was analyzed across product categories to identify which categories contributed most to overall profitability.

Technology generated the highest profit among the categories analyzed.

6. Profit by Region

Profit was compared across the four regions.

The West region recorded the highest profit among the regions analyzed.

---

💡 Key Business Insights

- Total sales reached 2.30 million with an overall profit of approximately 286.40 thousand.
- Sales increased from 2015 to 2017, with 2017 recording the highest annual sales.
- Technology was the strongest-performing category in both sales and profit.
- The West region generated the highest sales and profit among the regions analyzed.
- The Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier was the highest-selling product in the analysis.
- Regional and category-level analysis helps identify areas that can be monitored for improving future business performance.
